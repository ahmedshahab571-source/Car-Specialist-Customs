Immobilizer FAQs

Common questions about car immobilizer problems and our services.

A car immobilizer is an electronic security system that stops the engine starting unless the correct coded key or transponder is present. It communicates with the vehicle’s ECU to confirm the key is authorised, helping prevent theft.

Typical signs include a car that cranks but won’t start, an immobilizer or security warning light on the dashboard, a key or transponder that isn’t recognised, and faults appearing after a flat battery, battery change or key damage.
